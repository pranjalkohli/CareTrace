# Dialysis Machine Troubleshooting and Maintenance Guide

Applicable example models: RenalCare DX-700, DialyTech DT-500

## Safety and escalation

Follow the equipment manufacturer's service instructions and hospital safety procedures. Patient-care decisions must be handled by qualified clinical staff. Remove equipment from service when a fault could compromise safe operation.

## Arterial pressure unusually negative (AR-221)
### Possible causes
- Blood-line restriction or access issue
- Related connection, configuration, sensor, or component issues may produce similar observations.

### Diagnostic procedure
1. Inspect blood line for kinks.
1. Check access connection.
1. Verify clamps.
1. Follow clinical escalation procedure.

### Corrective action
Correct line restriction and escalate clinical issue as required.

### Escalation
If the fault persists after approved basic checks, document the observed code and findings and escalate to qualified biomedical/service personnel.

## Conductivity out of range (CO-115)
### Possible causes
- Dialysate mixing/calibration issue
- Related connection, configuration, sensor, or component issues may produce similar observations.

### Diagnostic procedure
1. Verify concentrate supply.
1. Check connections.
1. Run approved conductivity check.

### Corrective action
Correct concentrate supply or service calibration.

### Escalation
If the fault persists after approved basic checks, document the observed code and findings and escalate to qualified biomedical/service personnel.

## Air detector alarm (AD-204)
### Possible causes
- Air detected in blood circuit
- Related connection, configuration, sensor, or component issues may produce similar observations.

### Diagnostic procedure
1. Stop according to safety procedure.
1. Inspect circuit.
1. Follow machine-specific air-removal procedure.

### Corrective action
Resolve air detection condition before resuming.

### Escalation
If the fault persists after approved basic checks, document the observed code and findings and escalate to qualified biomedical/service personnel.
