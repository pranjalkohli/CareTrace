# ECG Machine Troubleshooting and Maintenance Guide

Applicable example models: CardioLab ECG-12, HeartTrace H-300

## Safety and escalation

Follow the equipment manufacturer's service instructions and hospital safety procedures. Patient-care decisions must be handled by qualified clinical staff. Remove equipment from service when a fault could compromise safe operation.

## Baseline drift in ECG (EC-301)
### Possible causes
- Poor electrode contact
- Related connection, configuration, sensor, or component issues may produce similar observations.

### Diagnostic procedure
1. Check skin preparation.
1. Replace electrodes.
1. Inspect lead connections.

### Corrective action
Improve electrode contact and repeat ECG.

### Escalation
If the fault persists after approved basic checks, document the observed code and findings and escalate to qualified biomedical/service personnel.

## ECG signal missing from one lead (LD-112)
### Possible causes
- Broken or disconnected lead wire
- Related connection, configuration, sensor, or component issues may produce similar observations.

### Diagnostic procedure
1. Swap lead.
1. Inspect connector.
1. Test cable continuity.

### Corrective action
Reconnect or replace affected lead.

### Escalation
If the fault persists after approved basic checks, document the observed code and findings and escalate to qualified biomedical/service personnel.

## Paper feed failure (PF-208)
### Possible causes
- Paper-feed mechanism obstruction
- Related connection, configuration, sensor, or component issues may produce similar observations.

### Diagnostic procedure
1. Check paper alignment.
1. Inspect feed path.
1. Remove obstruction.

### Corrective action
Reload paper or service feed mechanism.

### Escalation
If the fault persists after approved basic checks, document the observed code and findings and escalate to qualified biomedical/service personnel.
