# Ventilator Troubleshooting and Maintenance Guide

Applicable example models: MedAir V-800, RespiraTech RV-500

## Safety and escalation

Follow the equipment manufacturer's service instructions and hospital safety procedures. Patient-care decisions must be handled by qualified clinical staff. Remove equipment from service when a fault could compromise safe operation.

## High airway pressure alarm (AP-217)
### Possible causes
- Blocked expiratory filter
- Related connection, configuration, sensor, or component issues may produce similar observations.

### Diagnostic procedure
1. Inspect patient connection.
1. Inspect breathing circuit for kinks/obstruction.
1. Inspect expiratory filter.
1. Verify pressure settings.

### Corrective action
Replace blocked expiratory filter.

### Escalation
If the fault persists after approved basic checks, document the observed code and findings and escalate to qualified biomedical/service personnel.

## Low minute volume alarm (MV-104)
### Possible causes
- Circuit leak
- Related connection, configuration, sensor, or component issues may produce similar observations.

### Diagnostic procedure
1. Check circuit connections.
1. Inspect tubing for cracks.
1. Perform leak test.
1. Verify cuff/circuit integrity.

### Corrective action
Repair or replace leaking circuit component.

### Escalation
If the fault persists after approved basic checks, document the observed code and findings and escalate to qualified biomedical/service personnel.

## Unexpectedly high tidal volume (TV-331)
### Possible causes
- Incorrect ventilator settings
- Related connection, configuration, sensor, or component issues may produce similar observations.

### Diagnostic procedure
1. Verify mode and tidal-volume setting.
1. Check pressure support.
1. Run device self-test.

### Corrective action
Correct configuration and repeat verification.

### Escalation
If the fault persists after approved basic checks, document the observed code and findings and escalate to qualified biomedical/service personnel.

## Ventilator fails self-test (ST-009)
### Possible causes
- Flow sensor calibration drift
- Related connection, configuration, sensor, or component issues may produce similar observations.

### Diagnostic procedure
1. Inspect flow sensor.
1. Clean/reseat sensor.
1. Run calibration procedure.

### Corrective action
Calibrate or replace flow sensor.

### Escalation
If the fault persists after approved basic checks, document the observed code and findings and escalate to qualified biomedical/service personnel.
