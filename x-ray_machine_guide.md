# X-ray Machine Troubleshooting and Maintenance Guide

Applicable example models: Radiant Imaging XR-500, MedRay MX-900

## Safety and escalation

Follow the equipment manufacturer's service instructions and hospital safety procedures. Patient-care decisions must be handled by qualified clinical staff. Remove equipment from service when a fault could compromise safe operation.

## Exposure does not initiate (XR-102)
### Possible causes
- Interlock condition not satisfied
- Related connection, configuration, sensor, or component issues may produce similar observations.

### Diagnostic procedure
1. Check door/interlock status.
1. Verify system ready state.
1. Review displayed fault code.

### Corrective action
Resolve interlock condition before exposure.

### Escalation
If the fault persists after approved basic checks, document the observed code and findings and escalate to qualified biomedical/service personnel.

## Image appears underexposed (IM-214)
### Possible causes
- Incorrect exposure settings or detector issue
- Related connection, configuration, sensor, or component issues may produce similar observations.

### Diagnostic procedure
1. Verify kVp/mAs settings.
1. Check detector status.
1. Review exposure index.

### Corrective action
Correct exposure parameters or service detector.

### Escalation
If the fault persists after approved basic checks, document the observed code and findings and escalate to qualified biomedical/service personnel.

## Tube overheating warning (TH-330)
### Possible causes
- High accumulated tube load
- Related connection, configuration, sensor, or component issues may produce similar observations.

### Diagnostic procedure
1. Review exposure history.
1. Allow manufacturer-specified cooling period.
1. Check cooling system.

### Corrective action
Allow cooling and escalate if warning persists unexpectedly.

### Escalation
If the fault persists after approved basic checks, document the observed code and findings and escalate to qualified biomedical/service personnel.
