# Suction Machine Troubleshooting and Maintenance Guide

Applicable example models: SuctionPro SP-200, CareVac CV-100

## Safety and escalation

Follow the equipment manufacturer's service instructions and hospital safety procedures. Patient-care decisions must be handled by qualified clinical staff. Remove equipment from service when a fault could compromise safe operation.

## Vacuum level below target (VC-102)
### Possible causes
- Leak in tubing or collection system
- Related connection, configuration, sensor, or component issues may produce similar observations.

### Diagnostic procedure
1. Inspect tubing.
1. Check collection jar seal.
1. Inspect connectors.
1. Perform leak check.

### Corrective action
Replace leaking tubing/seal.

### Escalation
If the fault persists after approved basic checks, document the observed code and findings and escalate to qualified biomedical/service personnel.

## Pump runs but suction is weak (SU-214)
### Possible causes
- Blocked suction filter or tubing
- Related connection, configuration, sensor, or component issues may produce similar observations.

### Diagnostic procedure
1. Inspect tubing.
1. Check filter.
1. Inspect collection jar.
1. Clear approved obstruction.

### Corrective action
Replace blocked filter or tubing.

### Escalation
If the fault persists after approved basic checks, document the observed code and findings and escalate to qualified biomedical/service personnel.
