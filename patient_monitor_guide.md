# Patient Monitor Troubleshooting and Maintenance Guide

Applicable example models: VitalSys PM-300, Cliniview CV-900

## Safety and escalation

Follow the equipment manufacturer's service instructions and hospital safety procedures. Patient-care decisions must be handled by qualified clinical staff. Remove equipment from service when a fault could compromise safe operation.

## SpO2 readings intermittently drop (SP-221)
### Possible causes
- Damaged or poorly positioned SpO2 sensor
- Related connection, configuration, sensor, or component issues may produce similar observations.

### Diagnostic procedure
1. Inspect sensor placement.
1. Check cable and connector.
1. Try known-good sensor.
1. Check signal quality.

### Corrective action
Reposition or replace SpO2 sensor.

### Escalation
If the fault persists after approved basic checks, document the observed code and findings and escalate to qualified biomedical/service personnel.

## ECG waveform noisy (EC-118)
### Possible causes
- Loose or degraded ECG electrode connection
- Related connection, configuration, sensor, or component issues may produce similar observations.

### Diagnostic procedure
1. Check electrode adhesion.
1. Inspect lead wires.
1. Replace degraded electrodes.
1. Verify grounding.

### Corrective action
Replace electrodes or affected lead.

### Escalation
If the fault persists after approved basic checks, document the observed code and findings and escalate to qualified biomedical/service personnel.

## NIBP readings inconsistent (NB-207)
### Possible causes
- Incorrect cuff size or cuff placement
- Related connection, configuration, sensor, or component issues may produce similar observations.

### Diagnostic procedure
1. Verify cuff size.
1. Check cuff placement.
1. Inspect tubing.
1. Repeat measurement.

### Corrective action
Use correct cuff and reconnect tubing.

### Escalation
If the fault persists after approved basic checks, document the observed code and findings and escalate to qualified biomedical/service personnel.

## Display freezes intermittently (DS-401)
### Possible causes
- Software/process instability
- Related connection, configuration, sensor, or component issues may produce similar observations.

### Diagnostic procedure
1. Check power supply.
1. Restart according to service procedure.
1. Review event log.
1. Run system diagnostics.

### Corrective action
Restart and escalate for service if recurrence persists.

### Escalation
If the fault persists after approved basic checks, document the observed code and findings and escalate to qualified biomedical/service personnel.
