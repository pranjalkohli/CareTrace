# Blood Analyzer Troubleshooting and Maintenance Guide

Applicable example models: LabCore BA-400, HemaTech HA-600

## Safety and escalation

Follow the equipment manufacturer's service instructions and hospital safety procedures. Patient-care decisions must be handled by qualified clinical staff. Remove equipment from service when a fault could compromise safe operation.

## QC result outside acceptable range (QC-301)
### Possible causes
- Reagent deterioration or calibration drift
- Related connection, configuration, sensor, or component issues may produce similar observations.

### Diagnostic procedure
1. Check reagent lot and expiry.
1. Run calibration.
1. Inspect QC history.
1. Repeat control.

### Corrective action
Replace compromised reagent or recalibrate.

### Escalation
If the fault persists after approved basic checks, document the observed code and findings and escalate to qualified biomedical/service personnel.

## Sample aspiration error (SA-118)
### Possible causes
- Clogged aspiration probe
- Related connection, configuration, sensor, or component issues may produce similar observations.

### Diagnostic procedure
1. Inspect probe.
1. Run approved cleaning cycle.
1. Check sample volume.

### Corrective action
Clean probe and repeat aspiration test.

### Escalation
If the fault persists after approved basic checks, document the observed code and findings and escalate to qualified biomedical/service personnel.

## Reagent level not detected (RG-207)
### Possible causes
- Reagent sensor or container placement issue
- Related connection, configuration, sensor, or component issues may produce similar observations.

### Diagnostic procedure
1. Reseat reagent container.
1. Inspect sensor.
1. Verify reagent identification.

### Corrective action
Reseat container or service level sensor.

### Escalation
If the fault persists after approved basic checks, document the observed code and findings and escalate to qualified biomedical/service personnel.
