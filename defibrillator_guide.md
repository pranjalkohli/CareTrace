# Defibrillator Troubleshooting and Maintenance Guide

Applicable example models: RescueMed D-500, PulseGuard PG-700

## Safety and escalation

Follow the equipment manufacturer's service instructions and hospital safety procedures. Patient-care decisions must be handled by qualified clinical staff. Remove equipment from service when a fault could compromise safe operation.

## Failure to charge to selected energy (CH-410)
### Possible causes
- Battery or charging circuit issue
- Related connection, configuration, sensor, or component issues may produce similar observations.

### Diagnostic procedure
1. Check battery status.
1. Connect to approved power source.
1. Run charge test.
1. Inspect charging indicators.

### Corrective action
Remove from clinical use and service charging system if fault persists.

### Escalation
If the fault persists after approved basic checks, document the observed code and findings and escalate to qualified biomedical/service personnel.

## Pads not detected (PD-119)
### Possible causes
- Poor pad connection
- Related connection, configuration, sensor, or component issues may produce similar observations.

### Diagnostic procedure
1. Check pad connector.
1. Verify pads are within expiry.
1. Replace pads if necessary.

### Corrective action
Reconnect or replace pads.

### Escalation
If the fault persists after approved basic checks, document the observed code and findings and escalate to qualified biomedical/service personnel.

## Self-test failure (ST-501)
### Possible causes
- Internal diagnostic fault
- Related connection, configuration, sensor, or component issues may produce similar observations.

### Diagnostic procedure
1. Record error code.
1. Repeat approved self-test.
1. Review service documentation.

### Corrective action
Remove from service and escalate to qualified service personnel.

### Escalation
If the fault persists after approved basic checks, document the observed code and findings and escalate to qualified biomedical/service personnel.
