# Infusion Pump Troubleshooting and Maintenance Guide

Applicable example models: InfuCare IP-500, MediFlow IF-700

## Safety and escalation

Follow the equipment manufacturer's service instructions and hospital safety procedures. Patient-care decisions must be handled by qualified clinical staff. Remove equipment from service when a fault could compromise safe operation.

## Flow rate lower than configured (FL-104)
### Possible causes
- Partial occlusion in tubing
- Related connection, configuration, sensor, or component issues may produce similar observations.

### Diagnostic procedure
1. Inspect tubing path.
1. Check clamps and connectors.
1. Inspect for kinks or occlusion.
1. Verify pump calibration.

### Corrective action
Replace obstructed tubing if required.

### Escalation
If the fault persists after approved basic checks, document the observed code and findings and escalate to qualified biomedical/service personnel.

## Repeated occlusion alarm (OC-201)
### Possible causes
- Downstream tubing obstruction
- Related connection, configuration, sensor, or component issues may produce similar observations.

### Diagnostic procedure
1. Check downstream line.
1. Inspect catheter connection.
1. Check clamps.
1. Replace obstructed tubing.

### Corrective action
Clear obstruction or replace affected line.

### Escalation
If the fault persists after approved basic checks, document the observed code and findings and escalate to qualified biomedical/service personnel.

## Air-in-line alarm (AI-115)
### Possible causes
- Air detected in infusion line
- Related connection, configuration, sensor, or component issues may produce similar observations.

### Diagnostic procedure
1. Stop infusion.
1. Inspect line and connections.
1. Prime tubing according to procedure.
1. Check air sensor.

### Corrective action
Remove air and prime/reseat tubing.

### Escalation
If the fault persists after approved basic checks, document the observed code and findings and escalate to qualified biomedical/service personnel.

## Battery drains unusually quickly (BT-310)
### Possible causes
- Battery degradation
- Related connection, configuration, sensor, or component issues may produce similar observations.

### Diagnostic procedure
1. Check battery health.
1. Verify charging cycle.
1. Inspect power connector.

### Corrective action
Replace battery if capacity is below service threshold.

### Escalation
If the fault persists after approved basic checks, document the observed code and findings and escalate to qualified biomedical/service personnel.
